Chapter one

The answer to prayers

Before he ever held drumsticks, Vicente already had rhythm. As he tells it: he grew chubby in mommy Daniele's belly, gave daddy Francisco lots of loving little kicks and loved listening to music.

In October 2023 he arrived, perfectly healthy. No signs, no scares. And ever since, in his family's words, he is

a dream come true, the answer to our prayers and the love of his parents' lives.

Chapter two

Only seven months old

Then the flood hit the city. In the middle of it, Vicente caught bronchiolitis. It started with a cough and a runny nose, and ended in the hospital.

  1. ArrivalAs soon as he got to the hospital, he was intubated.
  2. 5 days laterThere were no more signs of bronchiolitis. Even so, he could not breathe without the machines. He was fed through a tube and was unconscious.
  3. The discoveryHis airways were blocked. A baby born healthy who, according to the tests, should not have been able to breathe until then.

breathe in · breathe out

“We don't know how he breathed until seven months old. He is a miracle.”
What the family heard from the doctors

Chapter three

And he walked out healed

A seven-month-old baby, at risk of respiratory and cardiac arrest, given up on by the doctors. He went through three surgeries, with no certain diagnosis.

The hospital became home. His parents lived inside their car, taking turns: one stayed with him in the morning, the other at night. They knocked on other doors and looked for help in Pelotas and Porto Alegre.

But, as the family always says, the Lord of life was with him the whole time. And he walked out healed.

The doctors still speak of risk, because the surgery is not permanent. But with every perfect breath, the family sees the hand of God.

“Glory to a God who does miracles.”Testimony caption, October 11, 2024

After all, Vicente breathesfor the glory of God.

Chapter four

A year of life given back

On October 11, 2024, the family shared for the first time “a little of the living testimony that is Vicente”. In the comments, one question kept coming up: what did he have? The answer is on this page.

And from then on came the balloons, the smiles, the first steps and, of course, the first drum set.

Chapter five

God: “I'll give you a gift.”
Vicente: “Amen!”

After all of this, at the second church service they attended, his parents received a word: God had given Vicente the gift of playing the drums, and he would not even need school or lessons to learn.

The family watched it happen. Anywhere there was music, he was glued to the drums, tapping with his little hands, always trying to get closer.

  1. Age 1Grandpa gave him a plastic drum set. The result surprised everyone.
  2. Age 2He got an electronic drum kit. And he earned it, because his gift was amazing.
  3. TodayHis dad and his uncle are gospel singers, and Vicente already plays at their events. Every day he shows more experience, and he is already being invited to play in other states across Brazil.

“An instrument in the hands of the Creator.”
